Privacy Policy

Mr Priceless client portal · Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Mr Priceless client portal ("the portal", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. The portal is run by Mr Priceless in New Zealand for our clients, so they can see and follow up the leads we send them and book site visits and calls.

Information we collect

  • Account details – your name, email address and phone number, so you can sign in and we can contact you.
  • Lead and booking details – the enquiries we pass to you, and the site visits, calls and notes you record in the portal.
  • Calendar connection (optional) – if you choose "Connect Google Calendar" (or Outlook), we receive permission to access your calendar as described below.
  • Basic technical data – such as sign-in times and error logs, used to keep the portal secure and working.

Google user data

When you connect Google Calendar, you are asked to grant these permissions:

  • openid and email – to confirm which Google account you connected and show its email address in the portal.
  • Google Calendar events (calendar.events) – to show your existing events and free times when you book a site visit or call in the portal, and to add those bookings to your calendar (and update them if you move a booking).

We read your events only when you open a booking screen, and only to show them back to you. We don't keep copies of your other calendar events. We store the sign-in tokens Google gives us so the connection keeps working, plus the IDs of the bookings the portal created in your calendar so it can update them. These are kept on our secure servers (Supabase) and are never shown in the browser or to anyone else.

We don't sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers we need to run the portal. Staff don't read your calendar data except where you ask us for help, it's needed for security, or the law requires it.

The Mr Priceless portal's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we use information

  • To run the portal: show your leads, bookings and calendar, and send you alerts you've asked for.
  • To contact you about your account and our services.
  • To keep the portal secure, fix problems and improve it.

Who we share it with

We use trusted providers to host and run the portal, including Supabase (database and servers), GitHub (website hosting), and Google and Microsoft (only when you connect their calendars). They process data on our behalf. We don't sell personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you're a Mr Priceless client and for as long afterwards as we need to for legal and business record-keeping. If you disconnect your calendar, we delete the stored calendar tokens.

Disconnecting and your choices

  • You can disconnect your calendar in the portal at any time.
  • You can also remove the portal's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing us (below).

Security

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and calendar tokens are only accessible to our servers. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your rights in New Zealand

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to access and correct your personal information. If you're not happy with how we've handled a privacy matter, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
